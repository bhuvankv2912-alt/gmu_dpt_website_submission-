Programs & Courses — AIML

Our department offers undergraduate, postgraduate and research programs focused on building solid foundations in mathematics, programming, and modern machine learning techniques. Below you'll find program descriptions, sample core courses and a semester-wise curriculum outline.

Explore Programs

Programs Offered

We offer a range of degree programs and short-term certifications to suit different career paths.

B.Tech — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

4-year undergraduate program combining core computer science, mathematics and specialized AI/ML training — ideal for industry roles and higher studies.

Intake: 60 | Duration: 4 years | Degree: B.Tech
Learn more

M.Tech — Artificial Intelligence

Two-year postgraduate program with emphasis on research, advanced deep learning, probabilistic modelling and applied AI systems.

Intake: 18 | Duration: 2 years | Degree: M.Tech
Learn more

Ph.D. — AI & ML Research

Research program for students pursuing novel research in AI, ML, Computer Vision, NLP and Robotics with faculty mentorship and funding opportunities.

Admission by research proposal and interview
Apply

Professional Certifications
