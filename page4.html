University Admission Activities

Ensuring a smooth and transparent admission process

📝 Pre-Admission Activities

  • Publishing admission notifications and brochures
  • Distributing online/offline application forms
  • Providing career counseling and guidance sessions
  • Conducting entrance examinations (if applicable)

📥 Admission Process Activities

  • Collecting and verifying application forms and documents
  • Preparing merit lists or publishing entrance results
  • Conducting interviews / group discussions
  • Allotting seats and confirming admissions
  • Collecting fees and issuing admission receipts

📂 Post-Admission Activities

  • Issuing admission letters, roll numbers, and ID cards
  • Creating student records in the database
  • Conducting orientation and induction programs
  • Processing scholarships and financial aid applications
